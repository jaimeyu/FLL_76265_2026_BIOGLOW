# Marketing & Outreach (`doc/marketing/`)

Branding materials, logo concepts, team shirts design, community outreach logs, and sponsorship pitch documents for FLL Team 76265.

## Contents
- **Branding Guidelines**: Team colors, fonts, logo files, and shirt graphics.
- **Outreach Logs**: Summary of community presentations, STEM workshops, and demonstrations.
- **Sponsorship Letters**: Templates for reaching out to local supporters and businesses.
- **Parent Flyers (`parents/`)**:
    - **Core Values & Plus'ing Flyer (`parents/core_values_flyer.html`)**: Printable flyer featuring the 6 FIRST Core Values, Dr. Woodie Flowers' Gracious Professionalism, and a deep-dive into "Plus'ing" for team collaboration.
- **Student Handouts (`handouts/`)**:
    - **Season Goals Worksheet (`handouts/goals_worksheet.html`)**: Printable student activity sheet for setting realistic fun goals, capturing favorite Pokémon, and taking the team spirit pledge.
    - **Robot Design & Mission Sketchpad (`handouts/mission_design_sketchpad.html`)**: Printable engineering idea sheet featuring mission number, design iteration (v1/v2/v3), brief summary & strategy lines, designer name, full blueprint drafting grid with Technic beam ruler, mechanism checkboxes, field test logs, and team "Plus'ing" (+) collaboration box.
- **SPIKE App Parent Flyer (`spike_app/spike_app_flyer.html`)**: Printable one-page guide and QR flyer for parents, showcasing app features, built-in STEAM tutorials, and installation steps.
- **Fundraising (`fundraising/`)**: Sponsorship templates and fundraising documentation.
