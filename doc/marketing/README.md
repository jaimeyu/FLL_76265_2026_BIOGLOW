# Marketing & Outreach (`doc/marketing/`)

Branding materials, logo concepts, team shirts design, community outreach logs, and sponsorship pitch documents for FLL Team 76265.

## Contents
- **Branding Guidelines**: Team colors, fonts, logo files, and shirt graphics.
- **Outreach Logs**: Summary of community presentations, STEM workshops, and demonstrations.
- **Sponsorship Letters**: Templates for reaching out to local supporters and businesses.
- **Parent Flyers (`parents/`)**:
    - **Core Values & Plus'ing Flyer (`parents/core_values_flyer.html`)**: Printable flyer featuring the 6 FIRST Core Values, Dr. Woodie Flowers' Gracious Professionalism, and a deep-dive into "Plus'ing" for team collaboration.
- **Student Handouts (`handouts/`)**:
    - **Checklist Builder & Pokémon CRM Handout (`handouts/crew_resource_management_kids.html` & `.md`)**: Kid-friendly printable guide on how to design and build custom mission checklists, communicate via "Point & Call" (Shisa Kanko / Chatot Echo), navigate match clocks, and adapt calmly under pressure without rules or finger-pointing.
    - **Season Goals Worksheet (`handouts/goals_worksheet.html`)**: Printable student activity sheet for setting realistic fun goals, capturing favorite Pokémon, and taking the team spirit pledge.
    - **Robot Design & Mission Sketchpad v1 (`handouts/mission_design_sketchpad.html`)**: Single-page engineering idea sheet featuring mission number, design iteration, brief summary, designer name, blueprint drafting grid with Technic scale, and team "Plus'ing" (+) collaboration box.
    - **Robot Design & Mission Storyboard v2 (`handouts/mission_design_sketchpad_v2.html`)**: Double-sided 70s NASA retro edition featuring a playing field grid route map with robot launch coordinates & compass facing sidebar, two dedicated keyframe motion drawing boxes with Lego stud scales, a full 10-step autonomous code & sensor logic sequence table, and a 2-run field trial consistency debug log.
    - **Innovation Project & Biomimicry Worksheet (`handouts/innovation_project_worksheet.html`)**: Printable & digital innovation planner for natural habitats, featuring step-by-step problem identification, nature's superpower (biomimicry) mentor, interactive invention blueprint drawing pad, STEM technology checklist, expert sharing plan, and peer "Plus'ing" review.
- **SPIKE App Parent Flyer (`spike_app/spike_app_flyer.html`)**: Printable one-page guide and QR flyer for parents, showcasing app features, built-in STEAM tutorials, and installation steps.
- **Fundraising (`fundraising/`)**: Sponsorship templates and fundraising documentation.
