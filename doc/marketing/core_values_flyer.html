➕ Plus'ing: Our Secret Collaboration Superpower

Say "YES, AND..."

What is Plus'ing? Instead of shooting down a teammate's idea with "No, that won't work," you accept the creative spark and build on top of it: "YES! AND what if we also..."

🛡️ Safe to Speak Up

Nobody fears being called "dumb." When every idea is welcomed, even the quietest teammates share game-changing ideas!

🚀 Wild Ideas = Breakthroughs

The craziest ideas often hide brilliant engineering solutions. Plus'ing turns wild concepts into real robot mechanisms.

🏆 No Ego Battles ("Our Idea")

No fighting over whose idea won. Plus'ing weaves teammates' thoughts together so the solution belongs to the whole team!

❌ The Idea Killer ("No, But...")

Teammate A: "What if our robot had giant airplane wings?"
Teammate B: "That's dumb. Robots don't fly and it won't fit in launch."
👉 Result: Teammate A feels hurt and stops sharing ideas.

✅ The Plus'er ("YES, AND...")

Teammate A: "What if our robot had giant airplane wings?"
Teammate B: "YES! AND what if we fold them on springs so they snap open to sweep all the coral pieces into base?"
👉 Result: Together, they just invented a winning attachment!
🤝 Team Pledge: We build each other up, we celebrate effort, and we always Plus each other's ideas! ⭐ FIRST LEGO League Team #76265
