🎯 The Mission Brief

Trees give our planet fresh oxygen, store carbon, and shelter wild animals. Yet billions of live trees are cut down every year just for paper. In Session 6, our team asked: "Can we make paper or write without cutting down trees?" Your mission is to pick ONE specific problem, explain why it matters, design a solution, and explain how it helps!
