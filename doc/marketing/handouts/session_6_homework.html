🎯 The Mission Brief

Trees give our planet oxygen and shelter wild animals, but billions of trees are cut down every year just for paper. In Session 6, our team asked: "Can we make paper or write without cutting living trees?" Pick ONE specific problem, explain why it matters, design your solution on Page 2, and explain how it helps!
