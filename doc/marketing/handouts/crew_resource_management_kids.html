🧠 Why Do Pokémon Champions & Astronauts Use Checklists?

In the 2.5-minute robot match, your heart races at 150 BPM and arena music is loud. When adrenaline rushes, short-term memory fades. Trying to remember 6 program numbers, pin alignments, and motor directions from memory leads to silly mistakes. A checklist takes the memory weight out of your head so you and your partner can talk clearly and navigate calmly!

🛠️ THE 4 BUILDING BLOCKS OF EVERY MISSION CHECKLIST ANATOMY OF A RUN CARD
⚡ 1. Power & Program
Pikachu Check

Ensure the SPIKE Hub is powered on and the screen displays the exact mission program number or graphic icon.

Checklist Item: [ ] SPIKE Hub: Program #2 (Whale Icon)
Callout: "Program 2 selected!" → "Echo: Program 2 confirmed!"
🔧 2. Hardware & Tug Test
Machamp Check

Identify the specific attachment, check color-coded pins, and perform a 1-second physical tug test to confirm it's locked.

Checklist Item: [ ] Dual-Lift Fork locked + 2-finger tug test
Callout: "Dual Fork mounted, tugged and rigid!"
🧭 3. Launch Alignment & Path
Lucario Navigation

State which mechanical wall jig to place against the border, and visually verify the drive wheels are straight inside Home.

Checklist Item: [ ] Jig B flush East Wall, wheels straight, path clear
Callout: "Jig B flush, robot 100% inside Home base!"
🚨 4. Contingency (Plan B)
Tactical Metagross

Decide in advance what to do if the robot misses or models jam. Never invent solutions while the clock is ticking down!

Checklist Item: ⚠️ If arm jams > 10s: Abort & Retrieve to Home!
Action: Skip to Run 3. Clock is more valuable than 1 penalty!
👉 The Superpower Technique: "Point & Call" (Shisa Kanko)
Closed-Loop Navigation

Japanese bullet train conductors and cockpit crews use Pointing and Calling to cut human errors by 85%! Don't read checklists silently with your eyes. Use your finger and your voice:

Step 1: Point
👉 Co-Pilot Points
Co-pilot touches item on card with finger: "Program 2?"
Step 2: Touch & Echo
🤖 Pilot Verifies
Pilot checks Hub screen and speaks out loud: "Program 2 confirmed!"
Step 3: Check & Go
✅ Check & Clear
Co-pilot marks check: "Check! Clear to launch in 3, 2, 1!"

📈 How to "Plus" & Evolve Your Checklist After Every Run
🌟 Level 1: Rookie Card

Only has program number and mission name. Good for slow practice, but leaves pins and jigs to guesswork.

🚀 Level 2: Pro Card

Adds attachment name, color-coded pin slots, and the 2-finger tug test so arms never drop off mid-table.

🏆 Level 3: Champion Arm-Band

Laminated card taped to your arm or worn on a lanyard. Includes emergency abort triggers and match countdown marks!
