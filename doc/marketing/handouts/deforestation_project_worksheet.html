🎯 The Mission

Welcome to the FLL Innovation Project Challenge! Deforestation threatens forests, wildlife, and ecosystems across the globe. Your mission is to explore a specific problem caused by or linked to deforestation, design a creative real-world solution, and test whether it can work!
