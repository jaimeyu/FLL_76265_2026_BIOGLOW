## 🌊 The 2026 Innovation Challenge

> **The Challenge:** Teams must explore how technology, creativity, and research can help us better understand and protect natural habitats. Inspired by nature, students are asked to identify a specific problem related to ecosystems and develop an innovative solution using their STEM skills.

### 🧭 The 4-Step Innovation Framework for Students

1. **Step 1: Identify a Specific Habitat Problem**:
   - Don't just say "save the ocean" — pick a specific habitat (coral reef, deep abyss, kelp forest, mangrove, polar ice, estuary) and an exact living organism.
   - Describe what is harming them and why existing tools struggle to see or solve it.
2. **Step 2: Learn from Nature's Superpowers (Biomimicry)**:
   - Nature has 3.8 billion years of engineering R&D!
   - Find an animal or plant that solves a similar problem (e.g. shark riblet denticles, manta ray non-clogging filter lobes, deep-sea far-red bioluminescence).
3. **Step 3: Engineer a STEM Invention**:
   - Combine nature's superpower with modern technology (optical sensors, passive sonar, soft robotics, clean wave/solar energy, AI classification).
   - Draw an invention blueprint and outline how it works.
4. **Step 4: Share with Experts & "Plus" with Teammates**:
   - Plan an interview with a marine biologist, park ranger, or STEM engineer.
   - Use our team's *"YES, AND..."* Plus'ing superpower to level up each other's ideas!
