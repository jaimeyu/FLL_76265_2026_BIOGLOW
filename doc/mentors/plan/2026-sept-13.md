# FLL Session 1: Building the Team & The Field

**Date:** Sunday, September 13 (and every Sunday until Christmas)
**Time:** 1:00 PM - 4:00 PM
**Attendees:** 9 Kids, Mentors/Coaches

---

## 📌 Coach's Prep & Presentation Strategy
*   **Keep it brief:** Kids tune out after 10-15 minutes of talking. Introduce concepts quickly and reinforce them *during* the activities.
*   **Prep the Blind Build:** Have 5 small, pre-built LEGO models ready, hidden in boxes or bags. Keep the models simple (10-15 pieces max).
*   **Materials Needed:** Paper/pens for goals, FLL field mat and LEGO mission model bags, 5 pre-built blind build models & extra loose matching pieces, Pokémon cards.

---

## 🕒 Schedule Breakdown

### 1:00 - 1:15 PM | Welcome & Introductions (Priority 1)
*   **Icebreaker:** Sit in a circle. Mentors go first to set the tone. Everyone shares their name, their favorite LEGO set (or favorite Pokémon!), and one thing they are excited about.
*   **The Golden Rule:** Introduce the big secret of FLL: *"We are not here to build a robot. We are here to build a team who happens to build robots."* (Priority 7)

### 1:15 - 1:30 PM | How We Work Together (Priorities 3, 4, 8, 10)
Keep this short and interactive. Have the kids stand up or raise hands for different points.
*   **"Plus'ing":** Explain that we don't shoot down ideas. If someone says "Let's make the robot fly," we don't say "That's stupid." We say, "I love that big thinking! What if we add tall wheels instead so it goes over things?" We *add* to ideas. 
*   **Failure is Awesome:** Ask the kids if they've ever messed up in a video game. Did they quit, or try again? Explain that in this room, failing means you are learning. If you learn why it broke, that’s a win.
*   **It's Not All LEGO:** Explain that an FLL team is like a real company. We need builders and coders, but we also need people to design a website, write presentations, research real-world problems, and make art. Everyone has a spot here.

### 1:30 - 1:45 PM | Secret Goals (Priority 2)
*   Hand out a piece of paper and a pen to every kid.
*   **Prompt:** Write down 1 or 2 things you want to learn or achieve by Christmas. (e.g., "I want to learn how to code," or "I want to be better at public speaking," or "I want to make new friends").
*   **Action:** Have them hand the papers directly back to you. *Mentors will use these to track and guide the kids individually throughout the season.*

### 1:45 - 2:15 PM | Team Building: The Blind Build
*   **The Setup:** Divide the 9 kids into 4 teams of two, and 1 team of three.
*   **The Rules:** 
    *   One person is the "Looker" and the other is the "Builder" (In the group of 3, two are builders).
    *   The Looker gets a pre-built LEGO model but must hide it from the Builder.
    *   The Builder gets a pile of loose pieces.
    *   The Looker must describe how to build the model *without* touching the pieces or letting the Builder see the original.
*   **The Lesson:** After 15 minutes, compare models. Use this to laugh at the mistakes (Failure is fun!) and talk about how important clear, supportive communication is.

### 2:15 - 3:30 PM | Main Activity: Building the Field (Priority 3, 6)
*   **The Task:** Open the FLL season mission model boxes. 
*   **Execution:** Assign pairs/groups to specific bag numbers. They must follow the instructions to build the models that will go on the competition mat. 
*   **Mentor Role:** Walk around and observe. Don't build for them. Encourage them to use "Plus'ing" if they get stuck. Remind them to learn how certain technic pins and gears work together.

### 3:30 - 3:45 PM | The Clean-Up Protocol (Priority 5)
*   **Stop exactly at 3:30.** No exceptions.
*   Explain that a clean workspace is a safe and efficient workspace. 
*   Assign specific roles: "Floor Sweepers," "Piece Sorters," "Table Wipers," and "Bag Organizers."
*   Make it a race—can the team get the room perfectly clean in under 10 minutes?

### 3:45 - 4:00 PM | Wrap-Up, Reflections, & Rewards (Priorities 6, 9)
*   **Circle Up:** Everyone gathers back together.
*   **Daily Learning:** Go around the circle. Every single person (mentors included!) must share *one* new thing they learned today. It can be about LEGO, about a teammate, or about themselves. (Priority 6)
*   **Homework Assignment:** Assign a light task for the week (e.g., "Watch the season reveal video on YouTube and come back with one idea for our Innovation Project").
*   **The Reward:** Hand out Pokémon cards! Remind them that they earned these by doing a fantastic job cleaning up, and if they report back on their homework next Sunday, they get another one.
