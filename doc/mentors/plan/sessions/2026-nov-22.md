## 📋 Minute-by-Minute Session Plan

### 1:00 – 1:15 PM | Kickoff Stand-Up, OPSEC Micro-Lesson & Team Split (15 min)

* **Set the Tone (5 min):**
  * Celebrate why we're pivoting: *We spent our earlier sessions really learning and brainstorming, which makes us a stronger team. Today is a focused pit-crew sprint to get our robot shipping-ready and our team identity locked!*
  * Review the **8 Goals for Today** on the whiteboard so every student knows what "done" looks like.
* **🛡️ OPSEC Micro-Lesson: Why NO USB Drives? (5 min):**
  * Teach kids a real-world engineering habit: *In real tech companies and security teams, plugging random or unmanaged USB drives into laptops is how malware spreads and how files get lost or overwritten.*
  * **Team Rule:** All robot apps and assets go into **Google Drive** (and GitHub) with clear version names—**never on USB sticks**.
* **Assign Tiger Teams (5 min):**
  * **Tiger Team A (Robot, Attachments, Jigs & Apps):** Builders & Coders focused on shipping readiness.
  * **Tiger Team B (Logo, Checklists, Celly & Bag-and-Tag):** Designers, Logistics Leads & Hype Crew.

---

### 1:15 – 2:20 PM | Sprint Block 1: Parallel Tiger Teams (65 min)

#### 🤖 Tiger Team A — Robot Shipping Readiness, Attachments & Jigs
* **Robot Base Inspection (20 min):**
  * Check all structural pins, cable routing, sensor mounting, and wheel stability so nothing shakes loose during shipping.
  * Confirm the robot fits cleanly inside the FLL inspection area dimensions.
* **Attachment Planning & Build (25 min):**
  * List every mission run and its required attachment (quick-swap mechanism, pin points, motor port).
  * Build/reinforce priority attachments now; clearly document any attachment work assigned for **take-home robot prep**.
* **Alignment Jigs (20 min):**
  * Build and test starting-box jigs (left and/or right home areas) so drivers can place the robot identically every single match.
  * Label each jig clearly (`JIG-L`, `JIG-R`, or by Run #).

#### 🎨 Tiger Team B — Finalize Logo, Competition Checklists & Team Celly
* **Finalize Team Logo (25 min):**
  * Review final logo candidates, make any last tiny tweaks, and lock the **official final version**.
  * Export/upload final logo file to Google Drive (`Team76265_Logo_FINAL`).
* **Competition Checklists (20 min):**
  * Review and customize our competition-day checklists:
    * Pre-Match Pit Checklist (battery %, wheel wipe, jigs, attachments, hub slot check).
    * Packing & Shipping Checklist (every item accounted for).
    * Review [Competition Mentor CRM Checklist](../guides/mentor_competition_crm_checklist.md).
* **Team Celly Brainstorm! (20 min):**
  * Brainstorm and pick our signature **Team Celly** (celebration move/chant for after robot runs, high scores, and judging rooms!).
  * Keep it fun, inclusive (all 9 kids can do it together), safe in tight pit/table spaces, and full of *Gracious Professionalism*®.
