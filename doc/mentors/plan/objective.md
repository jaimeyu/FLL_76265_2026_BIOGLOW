# Goals
1. FUN
2. Add to depth of knowledge
3. Build robots


# Strategies
- Culture activites for first 15 minutes to have kids get used to each other and learn to communicate. 
- When we split up into teams to do different work, I know we're supposed to have every kid try everything. One thing that makes this hard is knowledge hand off and I would like make it easier by having one or two kids stay behind to teach the others kids what they learnt. So its not just mentors lecturing or throwing them into the deep end. So maybe 10-15minutes of them helping out at the start and then we switched those kids back to their original group. Now they have to learn what they missed from their peers. I think this will help them get used to communicating and having knowledge being passed around intuitively rather than instructor led which is BORING.
- I want to know what each kid learned at end of session to track their growth to their goals at the start.
