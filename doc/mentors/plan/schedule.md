## 💻 Virtual Sessions Guide for Parents & Students

* **Platform:** Google Meet / Zoom (link sent via parent email / calendar invite).
* **Length:** 45 to 60 minutes maximum to keep focus sharp on school nights.
* **What to Bring:**
  * Notebook, pencil, and team folder.
  * Homework ideas or articles found during the week.
  * Chromebook or laptop with SPIKE App installed.
* **Purpose:** Virtual sessions protect Saturday in-person time for hands-on robot building and mat driving, while shifting research, discussions, and slide work to quick online touchpoints!
