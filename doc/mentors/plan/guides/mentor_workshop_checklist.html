Everyday Facilitation: Dividing, Conquering, and Hearing Every Kid

FIRST FLL #76265 LEGO LEGENDS
The Mentor Rule: Hands in pockets! Never leave a hand hanging in silence.
Acknowledge → Sequence → Active Wait Task → Divide & Conquer
1. PRE-SESSION SYNC (5 Mins Before Kids Arrive) Room Setup
Assign Mentor Zones (No Floating Ambiguity) Mentor A anchors Station A (Chassis/Build); Mentor B anchors Station B (Motors/Code). Decide who manages room supplies.
Identify Station Peer Ambassadors & Watchlist Designate 1–2 student ambassadors per station. Flag students who were quiet, frustrated, or struggled in the previous session.
2. LIVE ROOM OPERATIONS (When Multiple Hands Go Up) Queuing & Triage
Acknowledge & Sequence Publicly (Zero Waiting Anxiety) Call out names immediately so kids know they are on the radar and stop shouting. "Maya, I see you—you're #1. Joey, I hear you—you're #2. Sam, you're #3."
Assign an "Active Waiting Task" (Prevent Idle Frustration) Never let a student sit frozen while waiting in queue. Give them an inspection or self-check task. "Joey, while I spend 90s with Maya, do a 2-finger tug test on your beam and check your pin colors!"
Call Out Coach "Divide & Conquer" If Table 2 has multiple requests, alert your co-mentor loudly across the room. "Coach Mike, Table 2 has two in queue!" → "Roger, heading to Table 2 now!"
Redirect to Peer Ambassadors ("Ask 3 Before Me") Before answering mechanical or app questions, empower peer leaders. "Have you asked Alex, our Station Ambassador? Check with Alex first, and if you're both stuck, call me!"
Enforce "No Dual-Coaching" & Scan for Silent Strugglers Never have two coaches hovering over one table. Scan the room perimeter for the quiet child with crossed arms who hasn't raised a hand.
3. THE 2-MINUTE INTERVENTION PROTOCOL Don't Get Sucked In
Hands in Pockets! (Protect Kid Agency) Keep your hands off the bricks and the tablet. Point with guiding questions, never with your own fingers.
Step 1: Diagnose Expectations "What did you want the mechanism or code block to do just now?"
Step 2: Point to the Checklist or Guide "What does Step 2 on your Run Card say about axle length? Let's check the Technic ruler."
Step 3: Set 1 Micro-Goal & Walk Away! Give a 90-second testing goal, then immediately exit so they own the solution and other kids get helped. "Try testing this gear ratio 2x. I'll be back in 2 minutes to see what you discovered!"
4. MID-BREAK SYNC & CLOSING CIRCLE Session Debrief
30-Second Coach Huddle at Bio-Break Mentors sync: Who felt unheard or frustrated in Block 1? Do we need to rotate an ambassador to unblock Table B?
Closing Circle Voice Check Ensure every single student shares one discovery or win before leaving. Praise students who helped unblock peers!
