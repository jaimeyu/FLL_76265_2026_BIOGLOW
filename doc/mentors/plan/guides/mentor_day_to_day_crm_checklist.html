📋 Mentor CRM Day-to-Day Checklist

Operational Workshop & Inclusivity Protocol • Team #76265 "Lego Legends" • BIOGLOW

FIRST FLL #76265 LEGO LEGENDS
Philosophy: 👐 Hands in Pockets & Zero Unheard Hands!
Compass: "Not all obstacles are in your control, but your responses to obstacles are in your control."
1. Inspire: Who am I elevating today who hesitated or held back last week?
2. Recognize: Praise peer assistance, resilience, and debugging persistence.
3. Action: Queue publicly, ask Socratic questions, and let students hold the tools.
1. 🛫 Pre-Session Sync (15 Minutes Before Kids Arrive) Pre-Flight
Assign Mentor Zones (Divide & Conquer)
Mentor A anchors Station A (Chassis / Mechanical Assembly); Mentor B anchors Station B (Code / Sensors / Project). Mentors avoid clustering together.
Silent Struggler Radar & Watchlist Check FIRST Tip
Review students who seemed quiet, disengaged, or overwhelmed last time. Plan an intentional, low-pressure check-in within the first 20 minutes.
Unconscious Bias Self-Audit FIRST Tip
Examine internal assumptions (e.g. approaching boys for mechanical tasks and girls for presentation). Ensure equitable opportunities across all stations today.
2. 🤝 Arrival, Culture & Connection (First 15–20 Minutes) Connection
Low-Pressure Greetings & Eye Contact Respect FIRST Tip
Do not force physical contact; offer wave, fist bump, or verbal greeting. Never equate lack of eye contact with disrespect. Listen attentively regardless of gaze. Introduce yourself with preferred pronouns.
15-Minute Playful Warm-Up
Kick off with a low-stakes challenge (Blind Build, One-Hand Duck Build) to release school stress, spark laughter, and equalize confidence before tech blocks.
3. 🚨 Live Room Operations (When 3+ Hands Shoot Up) Live Triage
Acknowledge & Sequence Publicly (Zero Unheard Hands)
Never leave a hand hanging in silence. Announce queue numbers out loud to drop anxiety to 0%.
"Maya, I see you—you are #1! Joey, I hear you—you are #2! Sam, you are #3!"
Assign an Active Waiting Task
Keep queued students actively engaged while waiting for mentor support:
"Joey, while I spend 90s with Maya, do a 2-finger tug test on that gear shaft and check your axle length!"
Coach-to-Coach Closed-Loop Callout & No Dual Coaching
Call out: "Coach Mike, Table 2 has two in queue!" → "Roger, taking Table 2 now!" Never have two mentors hovering at one table.
Scan for the Silent Struggler
Scan for the quiet child with crossed arms or looking down who hasn't raised a hand. Check in on them first!
4. ⏱️ 2-Minute Socratic Intervention Protocol Hands in Pockets
Hands in Pockets & Rapid 3-Step Socratic Exit
1. Diagnose: "What did you want the robot to do?" → 2. Locate: "Where in the guide is that shown?" → 3. Launch & Exit: Set 1 micro-goal and walk away!
Answer Repetitive Questions with Fresh Enthusiasm FIRST Tip
Even if answered 20 times today, answer as if for the very first time! Maintain a warm, encouraging, unhurried tone.
5. 🔄 Peer Ambassador System ("Jigsaw" Handoffs) Peer Teaching
Station Ambassadors Teach Incoming Teammates
Ambassadors stay behind at their station to teach the incoming group. Mentors step back and let kids explain in their own words.
Reverse Handoff: Ambassadors Get Caught Up
When Ambassadors return to their home station, their teammates must teach them what was accomplished in the interim.
6. 🔄 Closing Circle & Blameless Plus'ing (Last 10 Minutes) Plus'ing
Every Voice Heard in the Closing Circle
Every student shares one discovery, test, or win. Celebrate students who unblocked a teammate today!
Blameless Plus'ing of Mistakes
Convert all errors into system upgrades: "Human error is a symptom of a weak system. How do we plus our jig or checklist for next week?"
💬 The 6 Micro-Affirmations Workshop Matrix FIRST® Guidance
Message Type Positive Micro-Affirmation (✅ DO THIS) Negative Micro-Message (❌ AVOID)
1. Verbal Gender-neutral terms: "Engineers", "Builders", "Lego Legends". "Hey guys", referring to builders as "he/him".
2. Para-Verbal Calm, unhurried, respectful, warm tone. Sounding annoyed, sighing when repeating instructions.
3. Non-Verbal Hands in pockets, kneeling/sitting to eye level. Hovering, slumping, checking phone, crossing arms.
4. Contextual "Who wrote this code?", "Who built this lever?" Assuming boys code and girls decorate.
5. Omission Full, patient attention to quiet or hesitant students. Only listening to dominant, vocal kids.
6. Praise Praising girls on torque/code; praising boys on notes/empathy. Gendered praise (praising girls only for neatness).
