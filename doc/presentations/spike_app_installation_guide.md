# 🤖 LEGO SPIKE App Setup Guide
### FIRST LEGO League Team #76265 — 2026 BIOGLOW Season

A step-by-step guide for student coders, builders, mentors, and coaches to download, install, and configure the **LEGO Education SPIKE App (v3.x)**.

---

## 📱 Slide 1: Supported Platforms

The **LEGO Education SPIKE App (v3.x)** is available across all major operating systems:

- **macOS** (macOS 10.15 Catalina or newer)
- **Windows** (Windows 10 / 11 64-bit)
- **iPadOS / iOS** (iPadOS 13+ / iOS 13+)
- **Android** (Android 9.0+)
- **Chromebook** (ChromeOS 90+ with Web Bluetooth)

## 🔌 Slide 3: Bluetooth Pairing & USB Connection

1. **Turn on the SPIKE Prime Hub**: Press and hold the green power button until the 5x5 light grid turns on.
2. **Enable Bluetooth Pairing**: Press the blue Bluetooth button on top of the Hub. The blue LED will flash rapidly.
3. **Connect in App**:
   - Open the SPIKE App project window.
   - Click **Connect** in the top left header.
   - Select **Bluetooth** or **USB**, choose **SPIKE Prime Hub** from the device list, and click **Pair**.

---

## ⚙️ Slide 4: Firmware Updates & Troubleshooting

### Firmware Updates
- When a new firmware version is released, the SPIKE App will prompt: *"Hub Update Required"*.
- **Important**: Keep the Hub connected via **USB cable** during firmware updates to prevent disruption.

### Common Troubleshooting Tips
- **Hub Not Discovered**: Restart Bluetooth on laptop/tablet and re-press the Hub Bluetooth button.
- **Program Not Running**: Verify motor/sensor ports match port letters defined in code (e.g. `Port A`, `Port B`).
- **Low Battery**: Charge Hub via USB until battery LED turns solid green.

## 🚀 Slide 6: Team Best Practices & Next Steps

1. **Backup Programs**: Save projects regularly to cloud storage or git (`code/missions/`).
2. **Use Clear Naming**: Name project files with mission IDs (e.g., `M01_DroneSurvey.llsp3`).
3. **Port Mapping**: Label physical robot wire ports clearly to match software code variables.
4. **Practice Readiness**: Ensure all practice laptops/tablets have SPIKE App installed and updated before team meetups!
