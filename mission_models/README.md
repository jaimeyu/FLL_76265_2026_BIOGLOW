# FLL 3D Challenge Mission Models & Attribution

This directory stores offline 3D digital CAD models (`.io`) and high-resolution rendered previews (`.png`) for FIRST LEGO League Challenge missions across multiple seasons.

> [!NOTE]
> The `.io` and `.png` files in this directory are external assets used for educational robotics simulation and are excluded from Git tracking via `.gitignore`.

## 📁 Season Directory Structure

The models are organized into season folders:

* **`bioglow/`**: Bioglow Season (M01 – M13, 13 `.io` models + 13 `.png` renders)
* **`unearthed/`**: Unearthed Season (M01 – M12, 12 `.io` models + 12 `.png` renders)
* **`submerged/`**: Submerged Season (M01 – M14, 14 `.io` models + 14 `.png` renders)
* **`masterpiece/`**: Masterpiece Season (M01 – M14, 14 `.io` models + 14 `.png` renders)
* **`superpowered/`**: Superpowered Season (M01 – M03, 3 `.io` models + 2 `.png` renders)
